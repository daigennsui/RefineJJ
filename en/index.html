07 — FAQGOOD TO KNOW

Questions
you may have.

Are you a travel agency? Can you make reservations?+

We are not a travel agency. We can work with trusted travel agencies, help arrange a private tour, or help you join a group tour. If you need a vehicle with special accommodations, we can help provide one.

How does the pre-trip consultation work?+

We usually meet online. The nurse who will accompany you on your trip leads the conversation so we can understand what support you need, including any medical assistance.

Does the nurse stay at the same hotel?+

For an overnight trip, please arrange a separate room for the nurse. If you need 24-hour assistance, we will do our best to arrange rooms close to each other. When overnight assistance is not needed, the nurse may stay at a nearby hotel.

Can I cancel my reservation?+

You can cancel your Joyful Journey reservation at any time. Reservations made with tour, transport or hotel providers follow each provider’s cancellation policy.

Do you offer travel insurance?+

We do not sell travel insurance. We recommend comprehensive coverage for trip interruptions, delays, illness or injury abroad and other unexpected expenses. We can refer you to travel insurance providers in Japan.

How can I pay?+

Major credit cards, including Visa and Mastercard, are accepted.
